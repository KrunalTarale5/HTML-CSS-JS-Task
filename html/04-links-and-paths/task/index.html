🔗 Your mission: A three-page mini site

✅ You'll know it works when:

  • You can travel between all three pages with the links and never see "page not found".
  • "Jump to contact" scrolls down, "Back to top" scrolls up.
  • The MDN link opens in a new tab.

Tip: open the code, find each ✏️ TODO comment, write your code, save, then refresh the browser (or use Go Live). Tick the boxes as you go.
