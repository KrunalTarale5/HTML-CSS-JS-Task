🔍 Your mission: Block and inline X-ray

Put on the X-ray goggles! This page draws a blue box around every block element and an orange box around every inline element. Block elements take a whole line. Inline elements flow like words.

✅ You'll know it works when:

  • Your three div boxes each take a full line (blue).
  • Orange boxes sit inside the sentence and only wrap their own words.
  • The "oops" example has a div on the outside and a span inside.

Tip: open the code, find each ✏️ TODO comment, write your code, save, then refresh the browser (or use Go Live). Tick the boxes as you go.
