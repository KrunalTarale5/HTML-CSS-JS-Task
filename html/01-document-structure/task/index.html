🚀 Your mission: Your very first page

Every web page needs a proper "cover" before the content. Finish the top part of this file (the <head>) and then say hello to the world.

✅ You'll know it works when:

  • The browser tab shows your own title.
  • You see your name as a big heading and a line about you.
  • In phone view (press F12, then Ctrl+Shift+M) the page is NOT tiny and zoomed out.

Tip: open the code, find each ✏️ TODO comment, write your code, save, then refresh the browser (or use Go Live). Tick the boxes as you go.
