Masala Chai ☕

Ingredients

2 cups water

1 cup milk

2 spoons tea leaves

1 small piece of ginger

Steps

Boil the water with ginger.

Add tea leaves and let it boil.

Pour in the milk.

Let it rise twice, then strain and enjoy.
