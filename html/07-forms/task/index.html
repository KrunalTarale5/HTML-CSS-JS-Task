📝 Your mission: Chai Club sign-up form

Build a real sign-up form. When you press Send, look at the address bar of your browser: it will show everything you typed, each one named by its name attribute.

✅ You'll know it works when:

  • Clicking a label puts the cursor in its input.
  • Pressing Send with an empty name or email shows the browser's own warning.
  • After a good submit, the address bar shows ?fullname=...&email=... with the things you typed.

Tip: open the code, find each ✏️ TODO comment, write your code, save, then refresh the browser (or use Go Live). Tick the boxes as you go.
