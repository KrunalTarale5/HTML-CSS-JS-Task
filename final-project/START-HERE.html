🏆 Your Portfolio Website

You have learnt HTML and CSS. Now put everything together into a website about you, one you can proudly show to friends, teachers and future employers.

What you will build

A one-page portfolio with a navbar, a hero, an about section, skills, projects and a contact area. It uses only HTML and CSS (no JavaScript needed). Here is the finished look:

Get started in 4 minutes

  1. Open the starter folder in VS Code (File, Open Folder).
  2. Right-click index.html and choose Open with Live Server.
  3. Open index.html and css/style.css side by side.
  4. Look for the ✏️ TODO comments. Each one tells you exactly what to write. Do them in order, saving after each one.

💡 The sample site is about "Aarav Sharma". Write about yourself: your name, your story, your skills, your projects. Replace the sample photo in assets/images/avatar.jpg with your own square photo.

What is inside the assets folder?

images/A sample photo (avatar.jpg), a desk picture, three project thumbnails and a favicon. Swap them for your own.
icons/Logos for HTML5, CSS3, Git, GitHub, VS Code, Chrome, Figma plus social icons (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, X, YouTube, WhatsApp, Gmail).

Make it truly yours

  • Your photo: square, around 600 × 600 px. Keep the file name avatar.jpg or change the src.
  • Your projects: build 3 pages from this course (invite, menu, gallery or your own idea). Take a screenshot of each: in DevTools press Ctrl+Shift+P, type screenshot and pick Capture full size screenshot.
  • Your colours: change the colour variables at the top of style.css (--brand, --accent) and the whole site changes. Try a palette from Coolors.
  • Your links: replace your-username in the GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram links with your real ones.
